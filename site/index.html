How it works

No labelling. No prompt-writing. The ground truth is what your people already do.

1 · watch

Mine the recurring jobs

Tacit ingests the actions your team takes — every comment, reply, triage, weekly post — and clusters them into playbooks: who does what in response to which trigger, how consistently, how fast.

2 · shadow

Draft in the dark, grade against reality

Each time a playbook's trigger fires, Tacit drafts what it would do and files it. When the human replies for real, the draft is scored against their reply. Trust is a Wilson lower bound — small samples are punished, not flattered.

3 · earn

Graduate one job at a time

Shadow → propose → auto. Recommendations are computed, never applied. A job that starts getting rejected or undone is demoted just as visibly. You always know exactly what it's allowed to do.

Backtest

Know what it would have done before it does anything.

Point Tacit at 90 days of history and it replays the shadow engine over all of it: drafts blind, scores against the replies that already happened. You see a per-job accuracy number, the misses, and the reason — before it is allowed to act. Nobody else can give you that number, because nobody else has the ground truth.

The trust ladder

Autonomy is a per-job property, not a global switch.

1CANDIDATE

Mined

A pattern with enough evidence: ≥3 occurrences, consistent replies, a recognisable trigger or a regular cadence.

→ you tap “shadow”. It costs nothing.
2SHADOW

Drafts silently

Every trigger becomes a draft nobody sees. The owner's real reply grades it within hours.

→ propose when trust ≥ 60% over ≥ 10 drafts
3PROPOSE

Asks first

Drafts land in the Inbox with a preview of exactly what will change — a diff for files, the text for posts. One tap approves.

→ auto when ≥ 10 approved, ≤ 10% rejected, 0 undos
4AUTO

Acts, reversibly

Runs on its own under a budget: writes per hour, dollars per day. Every action carries its undo. Three undos and it drops back to propose.

→ audited, replayable, exportable
Reversible by construction

Every write records its inverse. Undo a whole run in one click.

A comment on GitHub knows how to delete itself. A file write keeps the previous content. A Linear issue can be archived. Tacit's runs are transactions: commit, or roll back across systems, newest action first — and the rollback is on the audit log too. Anything genuinely irreversible (a shell command) is labelled that way before you approve it.

Install

One process. One database. Ten minutes.

1

Run it

git clone github.com/suncal/tacit
cd tacit && make setup && make demo

A seeded demo org opens at localhost:4800 with 90 days of history, mined playbooks and a backtest already run. docker compose up also works.

2

Point it at your team

TACIT_GITHUB_REPOS=acme/api
TACIT_SLACK_CHANNELS=C0BILLING
TACIT_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=sk-…

Polling, so no tunnels or app-store review. Or POST events from anything to /api/v1/events.

3

Mine, backtest, decide

Tacit shows you the jobs it found and what it would have done with last quarter's triggers. Put one in shadow. Come back in a week and read the trust score. Nothing acts until you say so.

FAQ

Questions worth asking

Isn't “similarity to what the human wrote” a crude score?

It's an explainable one — and it's calibrated against the only thing that matters: would the team have accepted this? Every score is visible next to the draft and the real reply, and a human can override any grade with a tap. With a Claude brain the drafts are genuinely good; the score tells you where they're good.

What does it do with jobs it can't learn?

It tells you. A weekly on-call summary full of live numbers scores badly in shadow, so Tacit recommends retiring the playbook — or connecting the data source it would need. Honest failure is a feature.

Does it need a model?

No. The local brain drafts from your team's own past replies and runs a deterministic planner, so the entire harness — mining, shadow scoring, trust, approvals, undo, audit — works with no key at all. Add Claude when you want drafts that adapt.

Where does the data live?

On your machine, in one SQLite file (or your Postgres). Secrets are environment variables, never rows. GET /api/v1/export returns everything as JSON.

How is this different from process mining or RPA?

Process mining tells you a pattern exists. RPA replays clicks. Tacit does the knowledge-work version — replies, triage, reviews, summaries — and proves each job in shadow against the human before it's allowed to act.
